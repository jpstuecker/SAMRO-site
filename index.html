We work with lower-middle-market and middle-market aerospace private-equity operating partners and portfolio teams, multi-site repair-station consolidators, corporate development leads, CIOs and IT directors, and transaction counsel coordinating TSAs and regulatory cutovers.

01

Protect certificate continuity

In a stock acquisition, certified procedures and shop-floor systems may need to remain in place; a rushed change can create certificate and audit risk.

02

Unify fragmented records

Connect siloed parts catalogs, rotable core-deposit tracking, and FAA Form 8130-3 records across acquired facilities.

03

Set controlled-data boundaries

Technical data and dual-use repair notes call for careful access design. Screening can inform export-control review, including deemed-export considerations under 15 CFR § 734.13.

04

Stay ahead of TSA costs

Long technical carve-outs can trigger step-up fees; legacy tables can also hide core-exchange receivables and vendor deposits.
