02 / PROJECT SOW

Turnkey sovereign integration pipeline

Move legacy records into a controlled data architecture, without replacing certified shop-floor software.

  • Bronze: Ingest raw legacy database dumps into a U.S.-person-administered environment on AWS GovCloud or Azure Government.
  • Silver: Segregate potentially controlled technical data for U.S.-person review; synthetically mask relational keys to prepare a commercial schema for offshore integration, subject to export-control review.
  • Gold: Load approved, cleansed inventory, financial, and rotable-core records to SAP S/4HANA, NetSuite, Salesforce, or Snowflake.
  • Multi-shop harmonization: Reconcile part numbers, interchangeability, and PMA / DER repair cross-references into one commercial sales catalog.
